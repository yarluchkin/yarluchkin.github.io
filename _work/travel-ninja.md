## Context

Rail Ninja sells train tickets in 50 countries. The partnership with Expedia meant hotels could be sold next to train tickets, but only through a booking product that met Expedia’s requirements.

## Problem

A hotel search has to show a lot at once: price, location, rating, room types and the rules of each rate. People compare many options and leave if paying feels risky. And the product had to pass an external review before a single booking could happen.

## What I did

- **Requirements.** Gathered them from the managers, analysts and the CTO, and turned them into a product scope that covered Expedia’s rules.
- **Search and results.** A results list with a map, filters and the total price per stay, so nobody has to calculate it.
- **Hotel page.** Photos, rating, amenities and location in one view.
- **Rooms.** Room options side by side: beds, size, view and cancellation rules.
- **Checkout.** Guest details, payment and a clear price summary on one page.
- **Inside Rail Ninja.** Built hotels into the train journey, so a traveller can book a stay right after buying a ticket.
- **Ownership.** I had no team under me and took responsibility for every design decision.
