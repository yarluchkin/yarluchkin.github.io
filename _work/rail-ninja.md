## Context

People often book trains in a country and a language that aren’t their own. Every carrier has its own classes, rules and data, and the interface has to make them look like one product. Today rail.ninja gets about 1.3 million visits a month, more than a third of them from the United States (Semrush, October 2026).

## What I did

- **Search and results.** Designed the search and the results, where classes, seats and prices for every train sit on one screen.
- **Seat choice.** Designed how travellers pick their seats on the train.
- **Destination pages.** Pages for routes, stations and countries.
- **Landing sites.** Designed sites such as [japantrains.com](https://www.japantrains.com/). It belongs to a network of more than 30 country rail sites that send travellers from search engines to Rail Ninja.
- **Components.** Date pickers, the traveller selector, maps and other parts, on the website and in the app.
- **Design system.** Prepared Rail Ninja’s components and patterns, agreed them with the product team and added them to the documentation.
- **Marketing.** Made social posts, banners and videos with the marketing team, in one visual style.
