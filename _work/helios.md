## Context

The app lives in the corner of the desktop, next to the system tray. It has to open quickly, show the state of the lamps clearly and stay out of the way.

## What I did

- **Research.** Collected references for hardware control apps and widgets, and wrote down what to keep and what to avoid.
- **Main flow.** A lamp screen with one large power button and a brightness slider, then a list of devices with their connection status, mood modes, a schedule and settings.
- **Modes.** Warm, cold, energise, calm, wake up, night, focus and romantic, each with its own icon.
- **Two themes.** Dark and light versions of every screen, both built on variables, so switching the theme changes no layout.
- **On the desktop.** Checked how the widget sits on a real Windows desktop, above the taskbar.
